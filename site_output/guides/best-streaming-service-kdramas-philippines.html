Best Streaming Service for K-Dramas in the Philippines

Updated November 2026

Which subscription should you get if you mainly watch Asian dramas in the Philippines? We checked every title in the DramaNet catalog against Philippines streaming data in November 2026 and counted which services carry them.

For K-Dramas, Netflix carries the most: 271 of the 857 we checked. For C-Dramas, Tencent Video carries the most: 13 of the 50 we checked. For Anime, Netflix carries the most: 16 of the 21 we checked.

A title can be on more than one service, so the shares add up to more than 100%. These counts cover our catalog, not each service's full library.
